---
name: Registrar Officer access policy
description: The user-approved scope of privileged administration and its visible role name.
---

All Registrar Officer accounts may use the capabilities previously limited to Super Admin. Display that privileged office role as “Registrar Officer,” not “Super Admin.”

**Why:** The user explicitly chose “All Registrar Officer accounts” after being told that this grants every account assigned that role account, record, settings, and privileged ticket-management powers.

**How to apply:** Grant the specific Registrar Officer role full management access, not every staff account. Receiver / Releasing, Records Management, Evaluator, and students retain their separate access boundaries.

Keep existing privileged accounts usable as Registrar Officers without replacing accounts or changing credentials. Role compatibility must apply to assignment eligibility as well as administrative access.

**Why:** The user requested a role rename, not account replacement. Treating an existing privileged account as Registrar Officer in the UI but excluding it from assignee validation blocked the user's handoff to Registrar.

**How to apply:** Treat legacy privileged accounts and current Registrar Officer accounts consistently when validating manual ticket assignments. Preserve the restrictions against assigning requests to students or inactive accounts.
